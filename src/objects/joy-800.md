Drawing on the Space Age, Joy emerges as a luminous sculpture. Its colours are handcrafted in
the atelier by layering epoxy resin to obtain ever-unique hues that interact with the dimmable light
and produce a blended glow. Two lamps are in the gallery, in soft green and pink; every other
reference in the chart can be ordered.
