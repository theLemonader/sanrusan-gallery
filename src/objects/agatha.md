Agatha is enlivened by an irregular circular top of transparent epoxy resin that holds a
dialogue with light. The colours are poured in several layers to create a deep, fluid compound,
embedded in a matte-effect bronze frame. The big top version stands alone in a room, entirely
handcrafted in the Como atelier.
