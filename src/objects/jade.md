Jade has an ethereal transparency and a reflective surface that plays naturally with light.
The brightly coloured top rests on a cylindrical brass base, whose distinctive veining comes from a
skilled etching treatment. The juxtaposition of ever-unique shades, blended in the atelier, with the
golden base makes it a precious piece. All the resin tops in the series are interchangeable.
