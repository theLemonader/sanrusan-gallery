Evolving from its namesake coffee tables, Cala is a coarse concrete base covered with thick
epoxy resin, evoking the lacustrine landscape of Como. The resin is cast in layers to recreate a deep,
shifting top that seems to reveal the lakebed underneath. The asymmetric, rounded shapes recall
shores smoothed by flowing water, and the segments can be set in a circle or alternated.
