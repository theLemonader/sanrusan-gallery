SANRUSAN is a gallery for functional art. We believe a chair can hold a body and a question with equal seriousness, and that the most considered work being made today is the work in which design has stopped pretending it isn’t art.

Transparency
Matters

SANRUSAN opens with a solo presentation by Draga & Aurel, the Como studio of designer Draga Obradovic and artist Aurel K. Basedow, and the first artists to show in the gallery.

Begun in 2019, Transparency Matters treats transparency as both a material and a method. Epoxy resin is poured by hand in successive layers over brass, bronze and concrete until colour seems to hang inside the object, changing with the light of the room and the position of the viewer.
