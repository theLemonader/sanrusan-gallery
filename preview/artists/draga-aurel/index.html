Draga & Aurel

Draga Obradovic, designer — Aurel K. Basedow, artist

Draga & Aurel is the studio of designer Draga Obradovic and artist Aurel K. Basedow, founded in Como in 2007. From an atelier close to the lake they work across art, collectible design and product design, and have never been interested in keeping the three apart.

The studio made its name with early collections such as Deshabillé (2007) and Heritage (2009), and with a way of reworking vintage objects through a contemporary eye. With Transparency Matters, begun in 2019, the work turned decisively towards material research.

Their references are precise: the essential volumes of Minimalism, the optimism of Space Age design, the perceptual games of Op Art, the culture of the 1970s. Resin, concrete, glass, bronze and brass are treated as active mediums rather than finishes, poured, layered and polished by hand until colour appears suspended inside the object.

Their work has been presented at Design Miami, Salon Art + Design in New York and the Salone del Mobile in Milan, and with galleries including Rossana Orlandi and Nilufar. Alongside their own editions, the studio has designed for some of Italy’s leading furniture houses.

At SANRUSAN — Exhibition 01

Transparency Matters

Draga & Aurel are the first artists to show at SANRUSAN, with a solo presentation of Transparency Matters in the gallery on Strada Zăgazului, Bucharest.

The selection brings together lighting, seating, tables and a room divider, each piece made to order in the Como atelier and different in the way only poured material can be. Some can be lived with, some can be walked around, all of them change as the daylight moves across the room.

Draga & Aurel’s Objects — In the exhibition

Agatha

Coffee table — Ø 34 × H 50 cm

An irregular disc of poured colour held in a matte bronze frame. Epoxy resin, bronze.

Lava

Coffee table — Ø 60 × H 37.5 cm

A thick resin top on a concrete cone, its surface eroded with sea salt until it reads like travertine.

Jade

Stool — Ø 40 × H 46 cm

A seat of blended colour resting on a cylinder of etched, veined brass. Epoxy resin, brass.

Cala

Bench — 110 × 42.5 × H 40 cm

Coarse concrete under deep layers of resin, after the shores and lakebed of Como. Steel base, burnished brass finish.

Joy

Wall lamp — 20 × 13 × H 160 cm

A luminous column of layered resin that owes its glow to the Space Age. Epoxy resin, satin brass, LED.

Reverso

Room divider — 29 × 12 × H 270–330 cm

A double-faced veil of cast colour that turns on its axis in a brass frame. Epoxy resin, semi-gloss brass.
